About the clinic

Dentistry that feels like a conversation, not a procedure

At Smile Arc, we believe dental visits shouldn't be stressful. Dr. Aravind and our team walk you through every step — what we see, what we recommend, and why — before any treatment begins.

Our Coimbatore clinic is equipped with digital X-rays, sterilised single-use kits, and painless injection technology, so care stays modern without losing the personal touch.

  • Digital X-ray diagnostics
  • Painless injection technique
  • Sterile, single-use kits
  • Emergency same-day slots
